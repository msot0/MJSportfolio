Creative Technology & Design

I'm a Creative Technology & Design student at CU Boulder, exploring graphic design, interactive coding, 3D modeling, and physical prototyping. Ideation and prototyping are my strong suits. I like being able to fully understand a problem and explore a variety of creative ways to solve it, in ways that leave a client happy and intrigue others. I'm especially drawn to developmental toy design, and I'm working toward a career where I can combine hands-on fabrication with playful, human-centered thinking.

I'm proficient in Figma, the Adobe Suite, SketchUp, and HTML/CSS/JavaScript, and I love the process of bringing an idea to life, whether that's through a sketch, a prototype, or a working interface. Outside of school, I also do audio design, mixing, and mastering for my job, so sound and music are a big part of how I think about creative work too.

I hope you enjoy looking through my work, and feel free to reach out with any questions or project ideas!

An interactive web app that lets users search for songs using the iTunes API. Results display album art, title, and artist, with a modal view for additional details and audio previews. Built with JavaScript, JSON data handling, and a finite state machine to manage smooth UI interactions. Helps users discover music in a fun and engaging way, showcasing my skills in web development and interactive design.

Duel Dash (p5.js Game)

A fast-paced two-player game built with p5.js where users can select a character and race against each other in a WASD vs IJKL keys setup. The game features simple mechanics, colorful graphics, and a fun competitive element. I implemented a finite state machine to manage game states like start screen, gameplay, and game over, ensuring smooth transitions and an engaging user experience. All iconography, graphics, and animations were created by me using Procreate.

3D & Physical Models

Laser Cut Acrylic Lamp

A geometric lamp I designed in SketchUp and laser cut from acrylic sheets. I designed the panels to slot together without any adhesive, so the joinery itself becomes part of the look. Since the panels are clear acrylic, the light passes through and reflects off all the angled surfaces, which was the main effect I was going for.

Built Spice Rack

A wooden spice rack I designed and built to fit a specific gap in my kitchen cabinets. My design changed a bit partway through the build as I adjusted the shelf heights and added the center divider, so the final piece is a little different from where I started, but that's part of the process. I like how it turned out and it fits exactly where I needed it to.

Spice Blend Packaging Concept

A conceptual spice blend package designed and 3D modeled to explore form, branding, and usability. The design focuses on structure, visual identity, and how packaging can enhance user interaction. Blending aesthetics and functionality to create a unique and engaging product concept.

Wooden Guitar Stand

A functional guitar stand designed and built using only 2x2 wood and screws. Created as part of a prototyping project, focusing on stability, simplicity, and efficient material use. Used SketchUp for 3D modeling the stand fully and its parts individually.

Recycled Go-Kart Concept

A playful go-kart concept inspired by Mario Kart, designed around the idea of using recycled materials such as aluminum cans. Focused on creativity, form, and imaginative vehicle design.
